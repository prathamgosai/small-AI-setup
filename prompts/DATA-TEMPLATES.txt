TIPS
------------------------------------------------------------------
* Ask one property / stock / pair per chat for the best answers.
* Keep "thinking" ON (don't start with the "fast" option).
* Always check the maths with a calculator before you invest.
* No AI can predict markets 100%. Use it as a second opinion,
  not as a guarantee.
